# DEAD-ENDS — what is closed, what it actually proves, what would re-open it

Copy this file into a project that is doing science or promotion. Fill rows from **this**
project's measurements. Do not copy another project's rows.

**Purpose: stop two opposite errors.** Understating a negative lets the team retread
refuted ground. Overstating a negative closes doors that were never shut — and that is
the more expensive failure because it is invisible.

Every row carries a **SCOPE GUARD**: the claim the evidence supports, and the claim it
does NOT. Cite the scope guard whenever you cite the row.

| # | Closed thing | Measured basis | SCOPE GUARD — what it does NOT prove | Re-opens if |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## How to classify a row

- **SOUND** — measured on the live ship path, or against real foreign bytes, or purely
  offline with no self-similar opponent pool.
- **UNKNOWN** — underpowered, or the ship path never carried the logic. Not refuted.
- **VOID** — the basis dies to self-similarity, wrong object, or vacuous counters.

Underpowered is not refuted. Scale N instead of killing the lane.
Arithmetic (teacher below the wall, etc.) can close a door independently of cell count.

## Anti-pattern

Do not cite one failed implementation as a closed **mechanism class**.
