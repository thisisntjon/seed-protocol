# HANDOFF — cold-open memo

Write into `workflow/handoffs/<date>.md` before ending a session. Written to be opened cold,
weeks later, by someone who has forgotten everything. Target: successor productive in ≤15
minutes. Health metric: each handoff shorter than the last.

```
# RESUME HERE — written <date>

## THE WHOLE JOB, IN FOUR LINES
1. <the single most important thing to do or protect>
2. <the second>
3. <the third>
4. <the fourth — if you need five lines, the project has a focus problem>

## BINDING RULES RIGHT NOW
<anything that must not happen, with the reason — a DO-NOT list beats a to-do list>

## EVERY NUMBER WORTH CITING
<measured values only, each with provenance (file/receipt + date). Anything not listed
 here is not citable without re-verification.>

## OPEN QUESTIONS — do not let these die unrecorded
<numbered, each with what resolving it requires>

## WHAT NOT TO DO
<the tempting wrong moves, and why they are wrong>
```
